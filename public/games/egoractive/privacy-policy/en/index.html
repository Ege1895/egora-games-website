Privacy Policy

Egoractive — last updated: September 24, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Ege Kaya, operating under the Egora Games brand ("we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, and protects information in connection with the Egoractive mobile application (the "App"). Egoractive is a gym and personal training studio management tool used by studio admins, trainers, and members. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices. This policy should be read together with our Terms of Use, which governs your use of the App, including subscription terms.

2. Who This Policy Applies To

Egoractive is used by three types of accounts within a gym or studio ("Gym"):

  • Admins — the owner/manager of a Gym, who sets up the Gym and invites trainers and members.
  • Trainers — staff added by an Admin to manage sessions and members.
  • Members — clients of a Gym, added by an Admin or Trainer.

Each Gym's data is isolated from other Gyms. Admins and Trainers can only see data belonging to their own Gym.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Account Information

You or your Gym's Admin/Trainer may provide the following to create and operate your account:

  • Full name
  • Phone number (used to sign in — we send a one-time verification code by SMS, we do not store passwords)
  • Email address (used to send a one-time verification code when signing in by email, and for account-related communication)
  • Gender (optional)
  • Role within the Gym (admin, trainer, or member)

3.2 Gym Operations Data

To provide the App's core functionality, we store data related to how a Gym operates:

  • Training sessions, schedules, attendance, and confirmations
  • Membership packages, remaining session counts, and renewal history
  • Group classes and events, including registrations and capacity
  • Trainer performance statistics and reports
  • Studio expenses and estimated revenue (Admin-only financial data)
  • Member feedback submitted after sessions
  • Achievement badges earned by members
  • The Gym's logo image, uploaded by the Gym's Admin

3.3 Body Measurements (Health Data)

This is sensitive health-related data. With an Admin's or Trainer's input, the App can store optional body measurement entries (such as weight and body composition figures) for a member, used solely to track fitness progress within the Gym. Because this qualifies as health data (and as a special category of personal data under applicable law, including KVKK Art. 6 in Türkiye and GDPR Art. 9 where applicable), we only process it with the consent of the member it belongs to, and it is only visible to the member themselves, their assigned Trainer, and their Gym's Admin — it is never shared outside the Gym or used for advertising. This data is permanently deleted when the member's account is deleted (see Section 8).

3.4 Notifications

We use push notification tokens (provided by Apple/Google) to send session reminders, attendance confirmations, and other Gym-related alerts. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

3.5 Subscription & Purchase Information

Gym Admins can subscribe to Egoractive's Gym management features on a monthly or annual, auto-renewing basis, purchased through Apple's App Store or Google Play.

  • The subscription automatically renews for the same length (monthly or annual) unless it is canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID or Google account at confirmation of purchase, and again at the start of each renewal period.
  • Pricing is displayed in the App and on the App Store / Google Play listing before purchase, and may vary by region.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time from your device's App Store or Google Play account settings — subscriptions cannot be canceled from within the App itself.
  • Any unused portion of a free trial period, if offered, is forfeited once a paid subscription is purchased.

All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple/Google — we never receive or store your card details. We receive a purchase receipt from Apple/Google, which we use only to verify and activate your subscription status. Full subscription terms are set out in our Terms of Use.

3.6 Usage & Device Data

We use Firebase Analytics to understand feature usage in an aggregated, non-identifying way (e.g., which screens are used most). We also use Firebase Remote Config to safely deliver app configuration and text without a store update. Standard technical data (device type, OS version, app version, crash diagnostics collected via Firebase Crashlytics) may be collected automatically.

4. How We Use Information

  • To operate core features: scheduling, attendance, packages, reports, and billing
  • To authenticate you and keep your Gym's data secure and separated from other Gyms
  • To send session reminders and Gym-related notifications
  • To process and verify subscription purchases
  • To improve the App through aggregated, anonymized usage analytics
  • To provide customer support when you contact us

We do not sell your personal information to third parties, ever.

5. Data Storage & Security

Egoractive is built on Google Firebase (Firestore, Cloud Functions, Authentication). Data is stored on Google Cloud infrastructure and protected with access rules that restrict every account to only the data belonging to its own Gym and role. Sensitive operations (such as subscription changes) are handled exclusively by server-side functions, never directly by the app.

7. Data Sharing

Your data is only visible within your own Gym, according to your role. We do not share your personal information with other Gyms or unrelated third parties. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Egora Games, our users, or the public.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain account and Gym data for as long as the account is active. Members and Trainers can delete their own account at any time directly within the App (Profile → Delete my account); see our Account Deletion page for the exact steps. When you delete your account:

  • Deleted immediately and permanently: your name, phone number, email address, login credentials, and any body measurement / health data (Section 3.3) tied to your account.
  • Retained as part of the Gym's business record: your past session attendance, purchased package/payment records, and any feedback you submitted — including your name within those records — because Gyms have accounting obligations and rely on historical reports remaining accurate and unchanged after a member leaves. These retained records are no longer linked to an active, sign-in-capable account, and your health/body measurement data is not part of them, as it is fully deleted.

Gym Admin accounts and full Gym data currently cannot be self-deleted in the App. To request deletion of a Gym Admin account, contact us at the email below; we process such requests within a reasonable timeframe, except where we are required to retain certain records (e.g., financial records) for legal or accounting purposes.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent (e.g., for personalized ads, or for processing your health data) at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

10. Children's Privacy

Egoractive is designed for gym/studio administration and is not directed at children. Member profiles are created by an adult Gym Admin or Trainer, not by children themselves. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child's information has been provided to us in violation of this policy, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to remove it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this URL with an updated "last updated" date. Continued use of the App after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
