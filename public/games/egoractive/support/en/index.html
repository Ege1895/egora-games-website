Support

Egoractive — Help Center

How Can We Help?

We're here to help you get the most out of Egoractive, whether you're a Gym Admin, Trainer, or Member. Browse the topics below, or reach out to us directly by email.

Contact Us
For all support inquiries, please email us at egoragames@gmail.com. We typically respond within 1–3 business days. Please include your role (Admin, Trainer, or Member) and your Gym's name so we can help you faster.

Frequently Asked Questions

I can't sign in / I'm not receiving the verification code.
Egoractive uses your phone number to sign in — no password required. If you're not receiving the SMS code:
  • Double-check the phone number is entered correctly, including the country code.
  • Make sure you have signal and that SMS isn't blocked by your carrier.
  • Wait a minute and try requesting the code again.
  • If it still fails, contact us with your phone number's country and area code (not the full number) and we'll investigate.
I'm a Member — how do I join a Gym?
Members are added by their Gym's Admin or Trainer, not through self-registration. Ask your Gym to add you with your phone number, then sign in with that same number.
How does the free trial and subscription work?
New Gyms start with a free trial period. Once it ends, the Gym's Admin can subscribe to a monthly or yearly plan through the App Store or Google Play. All billing, cancellations, and plan changes are managed through your device's App Store / Google Play subscription settings, not inside the app. Trainer and Member accounts are never billed directly — only the Gym Admin's subscription controls access for the whole Gym.
I was charged but my subscription still shows inactive.
This can happen briefly right after a purchase while we verify the receipt with Apple/Google. If your subscription still hasn't activated after a few minutes, try closing and reopening the app. If the issue persists, contact us with your Gym's name and the approximate purchase time.
A member's remaining session count looks wrong.
Session counts are automatically deducted when a session is scheduled and refunded if it's cancelled within the allowed window. If a count still looks incorrect, contact your Gym's Admin first, as they manage packages directly; if you are the Admin and believe this is a bug, contact us with the member's name and Gym.
The app crashes or won't open.
Try the following steps:
  • Restart your device and relaunch the app.
  • Make sure your operating system and the app are up to date.
  • Free up storage space on your device.
  • Uninstall and reinstall the app (your data is stored in the cloud, not on the device, so nothing is lost).
If the issue persists, please contact us with your device model and OS version.
I have a suggestion or feedback.
We love hearing from Gym Admins, Trainers, and Members! Send your ideas to the email address below — your input directly shapes future updates.

I found a bug

Please describe the issue in as much detail as possible and send it to us. Include your role (Admin/Trainer/Member), device model, operating system version, and the steps to reproduce the bug. We take all bug reports seriously and aim to address them in upcoming updates.

Account & Data Deletion

If you'd like your Egoractive account and associated data deleted, please see our Privacy Policy for details, or email us directly and we'll guide you through the process.
