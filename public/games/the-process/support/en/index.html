Support

The Process — help & frequently asked questions

Need help with something not covered below?
Email us at egoragames@gmail.com — include your platform (iOS or Android), your in-game factory name (Settings), and a short description of what happened. We reply to every message.

Getting started

How do I play The Process?
Drag a Boiler onto the board, then a Refinery, then an Emitter, and connect them in sequence (long-press a node, then tap the next one). The interactive tutorial walks you through this the first time you open the App — you can skip it with the "SKIP TUTORIAL" link, or replay it later from Settings → Field Manual.
What does "Reboot" do?
As your stations run, they wear down (integrity drops). Rebooting resets the factory but grants a permanent output multiplier based on how much you produced that session — the more your factory degraded before rebooting, the bigger the multiplier gain. It's the core progression loop: build, degrade, reboot, repeat, growing a little stronger every cycle.
What is the Blueprint tree / Ascension / Aether?
Blueprint Points are earned from every reboot and spent on a permanent perk tree (accessible from the tree icon next to Settings) — these perks survive every reboot. Ascension is a deeper prestige layer unlocked once your multiplier is high enough: it resets the whole factory (but not your Blueprint tree or other long-term progress) in exchange for Aether, a second permanent multiplier, and eventually unlocks the exclusive Aether Conduit station.

Progress & saving

Is my progress saved automatically?
Yes. The App saves your factory to the cloud in the background using an anonymous account (no login required). If you reinstall the App or switch devices, your progress is restored automatically as long as you're connected to the internet when you open it.
My progress didn't carry over to my new phone. What happened?
This usually means the App couldn't reach the network the first time you opened it on the new device, or too much time passed and a local "fresh start" was created before the cloud sync caught up. Make sure you have an internet connection, force-close and reopen the App, and give it a few seconds on first launch. If it's still missing, email us with your old device's approximate last-played date and your factory name — we may be able to help from server-side backups.
How do I delete my data / start over?
Open Settings → Data → "Reset all progress" and confirm both prompts. This immediately erases your saved factory from our cloud database and starts a fresh game. This cannot be undone. See our Privacy Policy for full details on what data we store.

Purchases

I bought the Foreman Pack but don't see the benefits.
Open Settings and scroll to the Foreman Pack section, then tap "Restore Purchases". This re-checks your purchase history with the App Store/Play Store and re-applies the benefits (ad removal, exclusive skin, free boost/repair, +5 Blueprint Points) to your account. If it still doesn't show up, email us with your store receipt/order ID.
What exactly does the Foreman Pack include?
A one-time purchase that permanently removes banner ads, unlocks the exclusive "Foreman" skin, grants +5 Blueprint Points, and makes the Boost/Repair actions free with no cooldown (instead of watching a rewarded ad or waiting). It is entirely optional — the full game is playable without it.
Can I get a refund?
Refunds are handled entirely by Apple or Google, not by us — we cannot process them directly. iOS: reportaproblem.apple.com · Android: Google Play refund request

Ads

Do ads play automatically?
No — banner ads may appear on non-gameplay screens, but a video ad is only ever shown after you tap a "watch ad" button yourself for a specific, clearly-labeled reward (Boost or Repair). We never auto-play an interstitial ad.
I don't want personalized ads.
Where required by law, you're shown a consent choice the first time you open the App. You can also turn off ad personalization at the OS level: iOS — Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; Android — Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID / Opt out of Ads Personalization.

Notifications

How do I turn off notifications?
Open Settings → Notifications and toggle off "Production Alerts" and/or "Daily Reminder" individually. You can also disable all App notifications from your phone's system settings.

Language

Can I change the app language?
Yes — Settings → Language, switch between English and Turkish at any time.

Reporting a bug

If something crashed or looked wrong, email egoragames@gmail.com with:

  • Your device model and OS version (e.g. iPhone 15, iOS 18.1)
  • What you were doing right before it happened
  • A screenshot or screen recording if you have one

The App also automatically sends us anonymous crash reports to help us catch issues faster.
