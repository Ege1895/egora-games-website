Privacy Policy

The Process — last updated: 25 July 2026

This policy explains what data The Process ("the App", "we", "us") collects, why, and what choices you have. We built the App with a "collect only what the mechanics need" principle: there is no account creation, no email/password, and no real-name requirement anywhere in the App.

1. Who is responsible for your data (data controller)

The App is developed and published by an independent developer operating under the name Egora Games. For any privacy question, request, or complaint, contact:

Email: egoragames@gmail.com

We do not collect your real name, physical address, phone number, or email address anywhere in the App. The "factory name" you choose is a nickname you set yourself and can change at any time in Settings — do not enter your real name or other personal information into it, since it is visible to other players on the weekly leaderboard.

3. Legal basis for processing

Depending on where you live, we rely on one or more of the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract — saving your progress and delivering the core gameplay you asked for by installing the App.
  • Legitimate interests — crash reporting, security, and basic product analytics needed to keep the App working and improving it, balanced against your rights.
  • Consent — personalized advertising in regions where consent is legally required (EEA, UK, Switzerland); you can decline this and still see ads, just less tailored to you. Consent is collected through an in-app consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) the first time it is legally required, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. We share the minimum data necessary with the following processors, each of whom is contractually bound to protect it and to use it only to provide their service to us:

  • Google (Firebase, Cloud Firestore, AdMob, Google Play Services) — hosting, authentication, database, analytics, crash reporting, and advertising.
  • Apple Inc. — Game Center identity (iOS only, if you opt in) and App Store purchase processing.
  • Other players — only your self-chosen factory name and your weekly score, shown on the public leaderboard.

5. International data transfers

Our processors (principally Google) operate data centers in multiple countries, which may be outside your country of residence, including outside the European Economic Area. Where this occurs, transfers are covered by appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses, as implemented by our processors under their own data processing terms.

6. How long we keep your data

  • Gameplay data is kept as long as your anonymous account is active. It is deleted when you use the in-app "Reset all progress" option (Settings → Data), or on request (see Section 8).
  • Analytics and crash data is retained by Firebase according to Google's standard retention windows (typically up to 14 months for analytics), after which it is automatically deleted or fully anonymized.
  • Advertising identifiers are not stored by us; they pass through to the advertising SDK for each ad request.

7. Your rights

Depending on your location (this section reflects rights under the EU/UK GDPR and equivalent laws elsewhere), you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Correct inaccurate data;
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten");
  • Restrict or object to certain processing;
  • Receive a copy of your data in a portable format;
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing that occurred before withdrawal;
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

How to delete your data

You can delete your cloud-saved gameplay data yourself at any time, from inside the App: open Settings → Data → "Reset all progress" and confirm both prompts. This immediately erases your saved factory state from our cloud database and starts you fresh. For anything this in-app option does not cover (e.g. analytics history tied to your device), email us at egoragames@gmail.com and we will process your request within 30 days.

8. Advertising and the User Messaging Platform (UMP)

The App shows ads (banner ads, and only user-initiated rewarded video ads — an ad is never shown to you without your own tap on a "watch ad" button). Where required by law, you will see a consent dialog the first time you open the App asking whether you allow personalized advertising. You can change this choice at any time by clearing the App's local data or reinstalling it, which re-triggers the consent flow.

9. Children's privacy

The App is intended for a general audience aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age required by applicable law in your jurisdiction (13 in the US under COPPA, 16 in most of the EU unless local law sets a lower age). If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Turkey — KVKK notice

For users in Türkiye: under the Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK"), the categories of data described in Section 2 are processed based on the legal grounds in KVKK Art. 5 (performance of a contract you are a party to; our legitimate interest in providing and securing the service; and your explicit consent for personalized advertising where applicable). Under KVKK Art. 11, you have the right to learn whether your data is processed, request information about it, request correction or deletion, and object to results produced solely by automated analysis. You can exercise these rights using the contact details in Section 1.

11. Security

We rely on Google Firebase's infrastructure, which encrypts data in transit and at rest, and restricts database access through security rules scoped to your own anonymous account. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable, industry-standard steps to protect your data.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

13. Contact

Questions, requests, or complaints about this policy or your data: egoragames@gmail.com
